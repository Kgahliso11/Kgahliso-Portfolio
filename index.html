About Me

I am a third-year Diploma in Computer Systems Engineering student at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT), Pretoria, with a strong passion for how hardware and software work together to solve real-world problems. My studies have given me hands-on experience in circuit design and connections, programming, digital process control systems, computer networks, databases, and operating systems.

I have a particular interest in becoming a Field Technician — I enjoy the hands-on challenge of building, connecting, and troubleshooting circuits and electronic systems in the field.

I am multilingual, speaking English, Afrikaans, Sesotho, and Sepedi, which allows me to communicate and collaborate across diverse environments. I am currently looking for internship or vacation work opportunities where I can apply my technical skills and gain real-world engineering experience.
